Critical Prohibited Ingredients

This guide adheres to a specific and strict dietary protocol. All kitchen staff must review these rules before preparation. The exclusion of **coriander (cilantro)** and **asafoetida (hing)** is a critical deviation from standard Jain recipes.

Root Vegetables (Absolute Prohibition)

Prohibited Items: Potato, Onion, Garlic, Ginger, Carrot, Beetroot, Radish, Sweet Potato, Yam, Taro, Turmeric Root.

Chef's Note: This includes all forms (fresh, dried, powdered). Use raw banana as the primary substitute for potato. Powdered turmeric is permitted.

Specific Plant & Fungi Exclusions

Prohibited Items: Coriander (Cilantro), Spinach, Asafoetida (Hing), Mushrooms (all types), Yeast.

Chef's Note: The exclusion of coriander and asafoetida is non-standard. Do not trust external "Jain" recipes. Build flavor with cumin, mustard seeds, and green chilies.

Animal-Derived Products

Prohibited Items: Meat, Fish, Seafood, Eggs, Honey, Animal Fats, Gelatin, Animal Rennet.

Chef's Note: Verify all cheese is made with microbial or vegetable rennet. Scrutinize labels on processed dairy for hidden egg ingredients.
